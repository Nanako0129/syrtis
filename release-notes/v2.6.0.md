## Features

- **Glass tint: make the popover's glass less see-through.** [#491](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/491), [#496](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/496) — thanks [@Lanternko](https://github.com/Lanternko)

  On macOS 27 the popover is a Liquid Glass panel, and a light window behind it could make its logo and text hard to read. A slider now tints the glass: darker in dark mode, lighter in light mode, so the text stays readable over whatever is behind it. It starts at the current look.

  The slider is in Settings → Dashboard → Glass tint, and in Quick settings, opened with the slider icon at the top right of the popover. The first time, a card on the Overview points to it. Earlier macOS versions don't show it.

- **Scrolling over the 3D chart scrolls the page.** [#472](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/472) — thanks [@blankhoney](https://github.com/blankhoney)

  Scrolling over the 3D contribution chart used to zoom it, so the page couldn't scroll past it. A two-finger or mouse-wheel scroll now moves the page, and pinch or ⌘ + scroll zooms. A "Pinch or ⌘ + scroll to zoom" hint on the chart fades out while you zoom and comes back after 8 seconds without zooming.

  On macOS 27, opening the popover now brings Syrtis forward so a pinch reaches the chart, and closing it, with Esc for example, returns you to the app you were in.

## Fixes

- **Reset on the 3D chart kept the rotated angle.** [#472](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/472)

  It only refitted the zoom. Reset now returns to the starting angle too, and the hover text on Fit and Reset says what each button does.

- **Option-drag and right-drag moved the 3D chart up and down against the pointer.** [#495](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/495)

  The chart now follows the pointer in every direction.

- **Turning off Cursor usage sync could still contact cursor.com.** [#492](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/492)

  If sync was switched off while a sync was waiting to start, that sync still went ahead. It now sends nothing.

  If turning sync off can't delete the downloaded usage, Settings now says so in orange under the switch: "Cursor sync is off, but some downloaded usage couldn't be deleted. Restart Syrtis to retry." Restarting Syrtis tries the deletion again.
